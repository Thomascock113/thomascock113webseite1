WBGT

LOCATION Apple APP Store

YEAR 2024

PROGRAM Mobile App

An iOS app designed to help athletes plan outdoor activities based on heat stress. It provides real-time WBGT data, hourly forecasts for the next 24 hours, and a 7-day outlook for selected locations. Sport-specific recommendations and heat-safety guidance help users make informed decisions about when and how to train.

The application combines real-time WBGT measurements with hourly forecasts for the next 24 hours and a seven-day outlook. Location-based weather information enables users to assess current conditions and anticipate periods of increased heat stress before heading outdoors. Sport-specific recommendations and integrated heat-safety guidance provide additional context for interpreting the data and adapting training to changing environmental conditions.

The project combines interface design, data visualisation, and software development to transform complex environmental information into an accessible, user-oriented digital product. Particular emphasis is placed on a clear information hierarchy, an intuitive user experience, and the presentation of relevant data at a glance.
