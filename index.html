HALLO

My design process begins with a careful analysis of the site, its context and the needs of its users\. I see architecture not as an isolated object, but as part of a larger urban, social and ecological system\. Each project develops from a central idea that informs its spatial organisation, materiality and relationship with its surroundings\.

I place particular emphasis on clarity of concept, functional flexibility and the quality of spaces\. I aim to combine these aspects with a conscious approach to materials, climate and existing structures\. Sustainability is an integral part of my design process: I consider the responsible use of resources, low\-impact materials, energy efficiency and the long\-term adaptability of buildings\. In response to climate change and increasing heat, I focus on passive strategies such as shading, natural ventilation, vegetation, water\-sensitive design and the creation of comfortable microclimates\. My goal is to create architecture that responds to its context, reduces its environmental impact, encourages social encounters and offers lasting value beyond its immediate function\.
