RIEGERPLATZ

LOCATION Riegerplatz, Darmstadt

YEAR 2026

SEMESTER 6.

PARTNERS none

PROGRAM Urban Planning

Riegerplatz, as an existing urban space, is not being reinvented, but adapted to the climatic conditions of a changing city. Rising temperatures and increasing summer heat make the issues of shading, evaporation and surface materials a central concern in the planning of inner-city open spaces.

The design retains the square’s fundamental structure and identity, concentrating the necessary reorganisation on its periphery. The car parking, which was previously organised horizontally over a large area, is consolidated into a compact, vertical car park. This frees up space without fundamentally altering the existing character of Riegerplatz. A façade of open-set wooden slats forms a permeable envelope that integrates the car park into the urban space whilst ensuring natural ventilation. In addition, the façade is greened, thereby expanding the neighbourhood’s climate-regulating green space.

The space gained is unsealed and reinterpreted as a climate-active open space. A planted high albedo low emissivity surface reduce the square’s temperature rise. Vegetation and open ground areas complement the structural measures by providing shade and promoting evaporation.

At the centre of the new square is a water feature. It marks the transition between the existing Riegerplatz and the open space created by the redevelopment. Water is used not only as a playful element, but also as a tool for creating atmosphere and regulating the climate: evaporation and the fine distribution of water help to cool the area, particularly during summer heatwaves.

Riegerplatz thus becomes an urban space that retains its existing function whilst responding to climate change. Parking, vegetation, water and the surface are not treated as separate elements, but as an integrated system for adapting the public space.
